Dynamic Reading Soundtrack

About the project

A browser-based audio instrument that lets readers combine a book genre with an atmosphere to create their own reading soundtrack.

Open the finished instrument

Design scenario

Dynamic Soundtrack-er explores an ongoing soundtrack for an activity or environment, shaped by occasional but meaningful user input.

I apply this scenario to reading. As the story changes, readers can adjust the soundtrack to match their own interpretation of its mood.

Response to the scenario

Readers choose a genre and an atmosphere, with four sounds available in each category. They can preview individual sounds, play both layers together, adjust the volume, and repeat a combination while reading.

Random changes the sounds within the selected categories. This introduces variation while keeping the reader's chosen genre and atmosphere.
