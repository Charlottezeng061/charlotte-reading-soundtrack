Parallel Prototypes

Exploring soundtrack control for reading

I am exploring different ways users could control a soundtrack while reading. These three prototypes compare direct selection, sliders and controlled randomization.

01

Direct Selection

Users choose a book genre, atmosphere and intensity before starting the soundtrack.

View Prototype 1

02

Mood Mixer

Prototype 2 was inspired by A Soft Murmur, which allows users to mix different ambient sound layers and adjust their volume separately. I adapted this idea by allowing users to control the volume of the genre and atmosphere layers independently.

View Prototype 2

03

Controlled Random

Users generate a random soundtrack and can lock the sounds they want to keep.

View Prototype 3
