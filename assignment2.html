Parallel Prototypes

These parallel prototypes explore different ways of controlling and understanding a dynamic soundtrack for reading. The prototypes are divided into two areas of exploration: soundtrack control and soundtrack guidance.

Area 01

Soundtrack Control

This area explores different ways users can control a reading soundtrack. The three prototypes compare direct selection, independent volume control and controlled randomization. The aim is to explore different levels of user control while keeping the soundtrack simple to use during reading.

01

Direct Selection

This prototype uses direct selection as the starting point. Users choose a book genre, atmosphere and intensity before starting the soundtrack. This gives users direct control over each part of the soundtrack.

Area 02

Soundtrack Guidance

This area explores how different types of guidance can help users understand and choose sounds for their reading soundtrack. The three prototypes compare different ways of presenting sound choices, from simpler guidance to specific sound examples and descriptive labels.

04

Descriptive Guidance

This prototype adds a short description to each genre and atmosphere option. For example, Fantasy is described as "Dreamy and magical sounds", while Calm is described as "Peaceful and relaxing". The descriptions provide guidance while keeping the number of choices simple.

06

Descriptive Guidance

This prototype keeps the same sounds as Prototype 5 but replaces specific sound names with descriptive labels based on how each sound feels. For example, "Rain" becomes "Soft & Steady" and "Ocean Waves" becomes "Gentle & Flowing". This explores whether users can choose sounds through their qualities rather than knowing the exact sound source.
